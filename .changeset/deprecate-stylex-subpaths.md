[component] The 20 `*.stylex` export subpaths of `@astryxdesign/core` are deprecated. Each module's JSDoc now says what replaces it — a class map, a generated token module, or an internal seam that disappears with StyleX — so an editor shows the deprecation on the import. The subpaths keep every export, every name and every StyleX namespace until they are removed in a major release.
